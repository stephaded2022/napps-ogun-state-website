Official Member Onboarding

01. Verification

Once you register your school, our State Admin will review your details and payment. Once verified, your school will appear on the Official State Registry.

02. Accessing Your Portal

Visit the Member Login page. Your "Access Key" is the Phone Number you provided during registration.

LOGIN KEY
Your Registered Phone Number
03. Teacher Matching

Whenever a teacher or student teacher registers on our state platform, our system automatically scans for verified schools. If a match is found, it will appear in your Portal Inbox immediately.

04. Posting Vacancies

Inside your portal, you can post specific subject needs (e.g., "Physics Teacher"). This will highlight your school with a "Hiring" badge on the public service board.
